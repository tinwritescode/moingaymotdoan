# Psalms 93 — The LORD Reigns Over Every Raging Wave

## Story

Just five concise verses long, Psalm 93 launches the *The LORD Reigns* collection (Psalms 93–99) with a coronation fanfare: "The LORD reigns! He is robed in majesty; the LORD has clothed and armed Himself with strength." Because He reigns, the world stands firmly established and cannot be moved. Nor is His throne newly built—it was established from of old, for He Himself is from everlasting (93:1–2).

From that serene eternal throne, the lens drops to roaring chaos below: "The floods have lifted up, O LORD, the floods have lifted up their voice; the floods lift up their pounding waves." Yet towering infinitely above thunderous oceans and crashing breakers, the LORD on high is far mightier (93:3–4).

The closing line settles into solemn awe: the LORD's decrees stand completely trustworthy, and holiness adorns His house forevermore (93:5).

## Key Takeaways

**History is not drifting rudderless—the LORD reigns.** However fiercely the world trembles, God's throne has stood from eternity and nothing can unseat Him (93:1–2).

**Waves of chaos roar loudly, yet never outmatch God's might.** The poet never pretends the *floods* are quiet, setting them squarely beneath the feet of the One enthroned on high (93:3–4).

**God's absolute power is paired with unfailing truth and holiness.** Limitless power without faithfulness or purity would be terrifying; the LORD combines supreme might with rock-solid promises and spotless holiness (93:5).

## Deeper Meaning

**Jesus displayed the LORD's exact sovereignty over raging waters.** In the Old Testament, roaring seas symbolized chaotic forces and empires defying God. When Jesus stood in a storm-tossed boat and commanded wind and waves into instant silence, amazed disciples asked who He was that even wind and sea obeyed Him (Mark 4:39–41)—finding their answer right in verse 4 (93:4).

**The proclamation *The LORD reigns* rings again in Revelation.** When heaven's vast multitude roars like rushing waters and peals of thunder proclaiming "the Lord our God the Almighty reigns" (Revelation 19:6), it fulfills the triumphant theme launched here in Psalm 93.

## Notes & Nuances

**The *The LORD Reigns* psalms (Psalms 93–99).** Right after the heartbreak of David's collapsed throne in Psalm 89 and Moses' wilderness prayer in Psalm 90, Psalms 93 through 99 announce in unison that while human kings rise and fall, the LORD Himself remains true King of the universe.

**Rising cadence in verse 3 (93:3).** Repeating *the floods* three times (*have lifted up*—*have lifted up their voice*—*lift up their pounding waves*) mimics surging breakers slamming harder and harder against the shore, only to hush completely before the quiet majesty of verse 4 (93:4).
